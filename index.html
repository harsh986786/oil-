Queries & Verifications

How is it different from refined or expeller-pressed oil?

Refined oils are processed via extreme chemical solvents and heat exceeding 200°C. ImPressed extraction processes keep it fully pristine below room temperature without modifiers.

Why are cold-pressed oils more expensive than regular oils?

We extract slower using heavy premium organic wooden churners yielding low output quantities but exceptionally superior nutritional density profiles.

Can I use cold-pressed oils for cooking — even regular cooking and frying?

Absolutely yes. Cold pressed variants like Yellow Mustard and Groundnut have natural high thermal tolerance limits safe for daily boiling, sauteing, and deep Indian frying cycles.
